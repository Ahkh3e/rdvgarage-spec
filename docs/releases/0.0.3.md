# Release 0.0.3

Rooms and voice: a text chat for every crew and for hand-picked groups, a hold-to-talk voice channel in each room, and crew admins to moderate them.

## In scope

| Module | Scope in 0.0.3 | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Crew admins | The owner adds admins; owner and admins remove members, turn voice off or on for the whole crew, delete messages, cancel RDVs and remove pins | features/crews.md, decision 0028 |
| Chat rooms | A standing room per crew, invite-only rooms across crews, RDV rooms; text messages kept 7 days; unread counts; mute; local notifications | features/chat-rooms.md, decision 0026 |
| Walkie-talkie | Hold to talk in a room, several people at once, not recorded; voice off per crew by a moderator | features/walkie-talkie.md, decision 0027 |

Supporting changes: the `chat` schema and the crew role in `docs/data-model.md`, the chat and voice functions in `docs/api.md`, the chat and voice disclaimers and the terms version bump in `docs/disclaimers.md`, and LiveKit in `docs/architecture.md`.

## Out of scope

- Remote push for chat and the walkie (#33, 0.0.4), report and block, images and attachments, a headset or steering-wheel Talk button, and moving free services to paid ones (#41).
- Not started: streaks, car profiles, galleries, convoy and minigames (`docs/roadmap.md`).

## Status: built, not yet running end to end

Built, reviewed and merged in https://github.com/Ahkh3e/rdv-garage (tag `v0.0.3`), with the module list in `apps/mobile/src/modules.ts` there.

Voice has never run on a physical phone. The first build used a LiveKit server; it is replaced by Rendezview's own relay on the owner's server (decision 0029), which needs the relay secrets in the function secrets and a development build with the audio libraries.

Known limits for dev testing, with the issue that tracks each where there is one:
- No real audio, no native build, and no simulator or phone run of chat or voice yet (#59).
- Everyone must accept the new terms (v2) on their next open.
- A person who has just joined a room can take up to about 15 seconds to show by name when talking.
- The iPhone Live Activity has no Leave button, so Leave is on the room screen, and the Android foreground notification and Notifee's compatibility with this Expo version are untested.
- The iPhone `voip` background mode needs checking against Apple review guidance before store submission.
- A closed app gets no chat or voice notification until remote push exists (#33).
- Follow-ups on performance (the direct read of messages) are listed in https://github.com/Ahkh3e/rdv-garage/pull/16.
