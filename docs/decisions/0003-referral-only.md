# 0003 Referral-only access

Status: accepted

## Decision

No open signup. Users join only by invite from an existing member.

## Rationale

Exclusivity is core to the brand and keeps crews trusted. Growth comes through the Toronto scene's own networks.

## Consequences

Invites expire after 24 hours and can be revoked (decision 0009). There are no invite quotas by design. Referral chain visibility is an open question in `docs/product.md`.
