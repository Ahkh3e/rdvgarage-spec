# Design

## Brand

Dark, minimal, exclusive. Toronto car scene.

## Principles

- Dark-first UI; no light theme in v1.
- Restraint: few colors, one accent, generous space.
- The map is the hero surface.
- Copy is terse. Say "an RDV", never "a RDV".
